# Input Prompts Log

This document records the exact, original prompt input provided for the Decision Log build.

## Original Build Request
```text
Task
Build and deploy a small web product using React or Astro.
You may use any AI coding/design tools you want.
Deploy it publicly using GitHub Pages.
You have 60 minutes total.

Product
Build a simple app called Decision Log.
It helps someone record important project decisions and quickly understand:
• what was decided
• why it was decided
• when it happened
• whether it is still active
No authentication, backend, database, or persistence is required.

Required experience
Your submission must contain:
1. A convincing landing page (more important than product itself)
   - Explain what the product does
   - Make it feel like a real product someone might use
   - You are allowed to over-promise since its a marketing page
   - Include a prominent Try Demo action
   - The landing page should communicate clarity, confidence and momentum, feel credible to founders and product teams, and make the value of preserving important decisions immediately obvious.
2. The product demo
   - Clicking Try Demo should open the actual working app
   - Users should be able to create and interact with decisions
   - Data may disappear on refresh
3. A polished deployed experience
   - Responsive
   - No obviously broken interactions
   - Sensible content and states

Critical design requirement
- The finished product must not look AI-generated. Not even slightly.
- The product should feel intentional, restrained, coherent, human-designed, and appropriate for its purpose.
- You are expected to challenge and modify AI output rather than accepting whatever it initially generates.

Submission
Send:
• deployed URL
• repository URL
• File containing the ORIGINAL input prompts you gave to AI for build, just your ORIGINAL prompts, not AI responses.
• a short explanation of the decisions you made and what you deliberately chose not to build
```
